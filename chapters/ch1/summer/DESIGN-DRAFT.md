## 1. The season in three lines
- **Question:** Why does every buyer suddenly want 14 days?
- **Answer:** Vane lends to the buyers. They pay farmers late. Every farm in the valley runs out of cash at once.
- **The forced yes:** Spring taught "long payment terms are dangerous". The King's purveyor takes 120 sacks and pays with an Exchequer tally in 42 days. The player cannot refuse the King. Every buyer now asks for 14 days or more. The player must price the terms and finance the gap.
- **Span and play time:** 28 game days, four weeks. Expected play: 3 to 4 hours [Guessing: measure after the first full run]. Eight ideas that use money, price and a contract need one decision each, then a second use. Four weeks give each idea two uses.

## 5. The finale: The Purveyor's Tender
- **Format:** a three-way negotiation at the Midsummer Fair with the purveyor, Vane's agent and the player. Spring's finale was a court of claims. This finale is a negotiation with a hidden second offer.
- **What it tests:** every core idea once. SU1 the tally discount, SU2 the walk-away number and the swap, SU3 the restated books as evidence, SU4 which orders the farm can fill, SU5 the price curve, SU6 which segment to supply first, SU7 the pass line on the new crop, SU8 refusing the gift.
- **Pass rule:** the player wins the Tender when the final terms are above the floor computed from the player's own books and the player did not pay a gift. The player does not need the lowest price.
- **Retake rule:** a lost Tender can be replayed with new numbers, as the Court can.
- **Story change:** a win gives the King's order at fair terms and lets Edric's name stand. A loss leaves the farm solvent but with a note sold to Vane on bad terms.
- **Hook:** the Traveller says a skimmer works in a barn. Autumn opens with a harvest too big for one person.

## 10. Risks and what would change the design
1. **C4.01 to C4.03 contain errors.** SU5 cannot be built until the curriculum teacher fixes them. Fallback: teach demand by watching sales only, with no elasticity number.
2. **C12.11 is not written.** The contract half of SU8 and the Vane and guarantee thread cannot be built. Fallback: cut the penalty clause and keep the gift scene.
3. **Master plan ids and the coverage file disagree.** The plan's SU1 uses C2.09 and C5.01, which the coverage file puts in Spring. The Lead should choose one source of truth.
4. **Load.** Eight ideas plus six previews is the cap. If the first full run exceeds 4 hours or a fresh player recalls fewer than 6 of 8 ideas three days later, demote SU8 and SU6's second use.
5. **Tender is one scene type that does not exist yet.** If the platform cost is high, the Tender can run as a series of scripted offers on the existing scene player.
6. **The tally.** The master plan tags it [Likely] real history. A discount rate for selling it must come from the season data, not from a literal.
